Travel story

Stepping off the plane in Helsinki for the very first time, the crisp,
pine-scented air immediately made me feel like I had landed in another world.
Nothing quite prepares you for that deep, peaceful quiet you find once you leave the city behind and head toward the endless lakes and forests
—it’s the kind of stillness you can almost hear. I spent my days wandering through sunlit wooden streets,
warming up with strong coffee and fresh cardamom buns, and ending the evenings in a wood-fired sauna before taking a freezing,
heart-stopping plunge into the water. Even though I went in a little unsure of what to expect, the easygoing warmth of the locals and the sheer,
unhurried beauty of the landscape made me fall completely in love with the place before my suitcase was even unpacked.
